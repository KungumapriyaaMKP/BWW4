# Temple Wedding Invitation (1:1 Clone of Saath Studio)

A pixel-perfect, 1:1 reproduction of the **Temple** South Indian luxury digital wedding invitation from [saath.studio/invites/temple](https://saath.studio/invites/temple).

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

1. **Atmospheric Lamp Lighting Opening Sequence**:
   - Dusk Madurai Meenakshi temple gateway with traditional brass Kuthuvilakku lamp on rangoli / kolam.
   - Interactive lamp top: tapping/clicking emits dynamic golden canvas spark particles.
   - South Indian classical Nadaswaram & Flute background soundtrack (`theme.mp3`) starts playing.
   - Smooth radial gradient CSS mask expansion reveals the lit temple ablaze with hundreds of oil lamps.
   - 5 flickering wick flames and golden halo glow.
   - Seamless transition into the couple's hero view.

2. **Floating HUD Controls**:
   - **Language Toggle**: One-tap switch between **English** and **Hindi (हिन्दी)** with instant real-time translation of all titles, ceremonial wording, dates, schedule, and RSVP messages.
   - **Audio Toggle**: Ambient soundtrack play/pause button with soundwave indicator.

3. **Hero View**:
   - Couple (Tanya & Vikram) in traditional Kanjeevaram silk saree and veshti standing hand-in-hand on the ancient stone steps of Vandiyur Mariamman Teppakulam.
   - Illuminated floating stone mandapam and temple gopuram reflected in twilight waters.
   - Gentle bouncing "Scroll down" indicator.

4. **Interactive Gold Foil Scratch Cards**:
   - 3 gold foil metallic cards: **DAY** (14), **MONTH** (February), **YEAR** (2027).
   - Realistic canvas scratch physics with circular eraser brush, pointer/touch support, and particle trail.
   - Automatic snap-reveal when scratched past threshold (>42%).
   - Displays wedding time and sunrise venue details upon scratch, with an **Add to Calendar** button.

5. **Formal Wedding Invitation Card (Jharokha Temple Arch)**:
   - Auspicious Tamil sacred invocation **"உ"** (Pillaiyar Suzhi).
   - Concentric circular monogram seal stamp **"TV"**.
   - Traditional ceremonial phrasing with family lineages:
     - *Daughter of Smt. Lakshmi & Shri Raghavan*
     - *Son of Smt. Meenakshi & Shri Subramanian*
   - Event date and Kalyana Mandapam venue in Madurai.

6. **Our Story Timeline**:
   - Couple's romantic journey from college volunteering to marriage.
   - Clean vertical milestone timeline (*Fest 2021*, *2023*, *Pongal 2026*).

7. **Photo Gallery Carousel**:
   - Arched jharokha cutout photo frames.
   - Horizontal snap-scrolling carousel featuring 6 portraits with italic captions.
   - "Swipe to see more" hint.

8. **Celebrations Timeline**:
   - Alternating vertical timeline with central gold line and diamond milestone markers.
   - Thematic mood accents:
     - **Mehendi** (Sat, 13 Feb · 10:30 am · The garden courtyard) with floral swing art.
     - **Reception** (Sat, 13 Feb · 7:00 pm · The mandapam hall with dinner on banana leaves) with royal sofa stage art.
     - **Muhurtham** (Sun, 14 Feb · 7:30 am · The mandapam, at sunrise) with sacred mandapam art.
   - Dedicated **Add to calendar** button for each event (.ics calendar file download).

9. **Live Countdown Timer**:
   - Real-time days, hours, minutes, and seconds counter ticking down to the first wedding celebration.

10. **Location & Venue**:
    - High-detail mandapam venue illustration (`venue.webp`).
    - **Show the map** interactive Google Maps iframe toggle.
    - Direct **Open in Google Maps** external navigation button.

11. **Spotify Badge**:
    - "Plays on Spotify" couple wedding playlist badge.

12. **Interactive WhatsApp RSVP**:
    - Guest name input.
    - Interactive `+` / `−` steppers to specify attendance for Mehendi, Reception, and Muhurtham.
    - **Send on WhatsApp** button that automatically constructs and launches a pre-filled WhatsApp message.

13. **Sign-off & Footer**:
    - Blessings from the Raghavan and Subramanian families.
    - Monogram stamp & couple names.
    - Saath Studio floral watermark badge.

## 🚀 How to Run

Simply open `index.html` in any web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox), or serve using any local static web server:

```bash
# Python
python -m http.server 8080

# Node.js
npx serve .
```

Navigate to `http://localhost:8080` to experience the invitation.
